## CEFR

- Can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure, etc.
- Can deal with most situations likely to arise while travelling in an area where the language is spoken.
- Can produce simple connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest.
- Can describe experiences and events, dreams, hopes and ambitions and briefly give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans.

# Unit 1 - Relationships and Preferences
**Lesson 1 - The People We Connect With** - By the end of this lesson, students will be better able to discuss ideal friends and partners using relative pronouns who and that.
**Lesson 2 - Things I Appreciate** - By the end of this lesson, students will be better able to discuss things they like and don't like using clauses with it and adverbial clauses with when.

# Unit 2 - Working Life
**Lesson 1 - What Makes a Good Job?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss their opinions of jobs using gerund phrases as subjects and as objects.
**Lesson 2 - Comparing Careers** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss jobs using comparisons with adjectives, adverbs, nouns, and past participles.

# Unit 3 - Favors and Messages
**Lesson 1 - Could You Do Me a Favor?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss favors, borrowing, and lending using modals, if clauses, and gerunds.
**Lesson 2 - Can You Tell Her…?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to leave messages using indirect requests.

# Unit 4 - Stories from the Past
**Lesson 1 - What Was Happening?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe past events using the simple past and past continuous.
**Lesson 2 - What Had Happened?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to tell stories using the past perfect tense.

# Unit 5 - Living Abroad
**Lesson 1 - Life Abroad** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss living abroad using noun phrases and relative clauses.
**Lesson 2 - What to Expect** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe expectations using when and if clauses.

# Unit 6 - Problems and Repairs
**Lesson 1 - What's the Problem?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe problems using nouns and past participles as adjectives.
**Lesson 2 - It Needs Fixing** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss what needs fixing using need with passive infinitives and gerunds, and keep with gerunds.

# Unit 7 - Environment and Action
**Lesson 1 - Our Changing Environment** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss environmental problems using the present continuous and present perfect passive tenses and prepositions of cause.
**Lesson 2 - Taking Action** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss what they can do about problems using infinitive clauses and phrases.

# Unit 8 - Preferences and Learning
**Lesson 1 - I'd Rather…** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss personal preferences using would rather and would prefer.
**Lesson 2 - Learning Your Way** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss ways to learn and personal qualities using by + gerund for manner.

# Unit 9 - Services and Solutions
**Lesson 1 - Getting Things Done** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss professional services using causatives.
**Lesson 2 - Coming Up With Solutions** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to use three-word phrasal verbs and make suggestions using a variety of structures.

# Unit 10 - Past and Future
**Lesson 1 - Moments That Changed History** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss important past events using time references.
**Lesson 2 - Looking Ahead** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to make predictions using a variety of structures.

# Unit 11 - Milestones and Regrets
**Lesson 1 - Life's Milestones** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss milestones in their lives using a variety of time clauses.
**Lesson 2 - If I Had Known** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss regrets using should have + past participle and if clauses in the past perfect.

# Unit 12 - Views and Reasons
**Lesson 1 - What Success Takes** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to express personal views and describe qualities for success using infinitive clauses and phrases.

**Lesson 2 - Explaining Why** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe features and give reasons using because, since, because of, for, due to, and the reason.

# Unit 13 - Explaining the Past
**Lesson 1 - What Must Have Happened?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to give explanations, reasons, and suggestions using past modals must have, may have, and could have.
**Lesson 2 - What Could Have Been Done?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to give opinions and advice with past modals should have, could have, and would have.

# Unit 14 - Processes and Professions
**Lesson 1 - How It's Made** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe processes using the passive.
**Lesson 2 - People at Work** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss jobs using defining and non-defining relative clauses.

# Unit 15 - Recommendations and Opinions
**Lesson 1 - What Should Be Done?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to make recommendations using passive modals.
**Lesson 2 - Don't You Agree?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to express opinions using tag questions.

# Unit 16 - Accomplishments and Goals
**Lesson 1 - What I'm Proud Of** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss personal accomplishments using complex noun phrases with gerunds.
**Lesson 2 - By This Time Next Year** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss accomplishments and goals using future tenses, including the future perfect.
