## CEFR

- Can introduce him/herself and others and can ask and answer questions about personal details such as where he/she lives, people he/she knows and things he/she has.
- Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases aimed at the satisfaction of needs of a concrete type.
- Can interact in a simple way provided the other person talks slowly and clearly and is prepared to help.

# Unit 1 - Meeting People
**Lesson 1 - Hello** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to say hello and make introductions using my, your, his, and her.
**Lesson 2 - Stay in Touch** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to say good-bye and exchange contact information using subject pronouns and the verb be.

# Unit 2 - Things Around Us
**Lesson 1 - What's This?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to identify and discuss personal and classroom objects using the articles a / an, plurals, this / these, and it / they.
**Lesson 2 - Where Is It?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss the location of items using yes / no and where questions with be, the article the, and prepositions of place.

# Unit 3 - Around the World
**Lesson 1 - Where Are You From?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss cities, countries, nationalities, and languages using yes / no questions with be.
**Lesson 2 - What Are They Like?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss people's appearances, personalities, and ages using Wh- questions with be.

# Unit 4 - What We Wear
**Lesson 1 - Whose Is It?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss work and free-time clothes and colors using possessive adjectives, possessive pronouns, and possessives with names.
**Lesson 2 - Weather Watch** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss the weather and what people are wearing using the present continuous and conjunctions.

# Unit 5 - Time Around the World
**Lesson 1 - What Time Is It There?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss cities and time zones using time expressions.
**Lesson 2 - What Are They Doing?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss people's activities using the present continuous.

# Unit 6 - Family on the Move
**Lesson 1 - Getting Around** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss transportation and family using simple present statements.
**Lesson 2 - Every Day** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss daily and weekly routines using simple present questions.

# Unit 7 - Home Life
**Lesson 1 - Home Tour** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe houses and apartments using simple present questions and short answers.
**Lesson 2 - Dream Home** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss furniture, appliances, and dream homes using statements with there is / there are.

# Unit 8 - Working Life
**Lesson 1 - At Work** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss jobs and workplaces using simple present Wh- questions.
**Lesson 2 - Job Talk** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to give opinions about jobs using be + adjective and adjective + noun.

# Unit 9 - Food and Habits
**Lesson 1 - What's in the Kitchen?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss foods they like, need, and have using some / any and count / noncount nouns.
**Lesson 2 - Eating Habits** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to describe eating habits using adverbs of frequency.

# Unit 10 - Sports and Talents
**Lesson 1 - Game Day** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss sports they watch and play using simple present Wh- questions.
**Lesson 2 - Show Your Talent** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss skills, abilities, and talents using can and can't.

# Unit 11 - Making Plans
**Lesson 1 - Weekend Plans** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss evening, weekend, and birthday plans using statements and yes / no questions with be going to.
**Lesson 2 - Let's Celebrate!** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss plans to celebrate holidays, special occasions, and festivals using Wh- questions with be going to.

# Unit 12 - Health
**Lesson 1 - Feeling Sick** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss the body and common ailments using have + noun and feel + adjective.
**Lesson 2 - Feel Better** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss common remedies and give health advice using imperatives.

# Unit 13 - Around Town
**Lesson 1 - Shopping Around** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss stores, items to buy in stores, and store locations using prepositions of place.
**Lesson 2 - Which Way?** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to ask for and give directions to various locations.

# Unit 14 - Past Adventures
**Lesson 1 - Last Weekend** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss weekend activities using simple past statements with regular and irregular verbs.
**Lesson 2 - Vacation Stories** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss vacation activities using simple past yes / no questions and short answers.

# Unit 15 - Looking Back
**Lesson 1 - Family History** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss family and personal history using the past of be.
**Lesson 2 - School Days** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to discuss school experiences and memories using Wh- questions with did, was, and were.

# Unit 16 - Keeping in Touch
**Lesson 1 - Leave a Message** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to make phone calls and leave messages using subject and object pronouns.
**Lesson 2 - You're Invited!** - By the end of this lesson, students will be able to make, accept, and decline invitations using Do you want to ... ?, Would you like to ... ?, and verb + to.
